

# 🚗 Electric Vehicle Market Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **interactive Power BI dashboard** that analyzes the global Electric Vehicle (EV) market. It provides insights into **vehicle performance, battery technology, manufacturer trends, pricing, and sales growth**.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand:

* EV market growth over time
* Manufacturer performance
* Battery technology impact
* Cost vs performance comparison

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze EV market trends and growth patterns
* Compare manufacturers based on range, price, and sales
* Evaluate battery technologies and their efficiency
* Identify best-value electric vehicles

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

* 🚘 **Total Vehicles:** 3.02K
* ⚡ **Average Range:** 350 km
* 🔋 **Battery Capacity:** 84.31 kWh
* 💰 **Average Price:** 90.61K USD
* 📦 **Total Units Sold:** 30.85M

  * Manufacturer

  * Price (USD)

## 🔍 Key Insights

* 🚀 EV adoption has significantly increased over time
* 🔋 Lithium-based batteries dominate the market
* 💰 Premium brands offer higher range but at higher cost
* ⚖️ Some mid-range EVs provide better value (price per km)
* 📈 Sales peaked around recent years showing strong demand
